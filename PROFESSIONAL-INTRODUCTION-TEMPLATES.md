# Professional Introduction Template Library

These are speaking frameworks, not scripts to memorize. Replace placeholders with your own factual information.

## Template A — Fresher / Entry-Level

> Good morning, and thank you for the opportunity. My name is **[Name]**. I recently completed **[Degree]** from **[Institute]**. My primary area of interest is **[Data Science / Data Analytics / AI]**, with hands-on exposure to **[3–4 relevant skills]**.
>
> During my internship / academic work, I worked on **[Project]**, where my responsibility was **[actual contribution]**. I used **[tools]** to **[approach]**, and the key outcome was **[verified result]**.
>
> I also worked on **[second project]**, which helped me strengthen my understanding of **[relevant capability]**. I am particularly interested in this opportunity because it aligns with my interest in **[role area]** and gives me an opportunity to contribute while continuing to learn.
>
> That is a brief overview about me. Thank you.

## Template B — Experienced Professional

> Good morning. Thank you for the opportunity. I’m **[Name]**, and I have **[X] years of experience** in **[domain]**. Currently, I work as **[role]** at **[company]**, where I focus on **[business area]**.
>
> My primary responsibility is **[responsibility]**. In my recent project, **[project/problem]**, I worked on **[actual contribution]** using **[technology/tools]**. The work resulted in **[verified impact]**.
>
> Before this role, I worked at **[previous company/role]**, where I gained experience in **[relevant capability]**. Across these roles, I have developed strengths in **[3–4 strengths]**.
>
> I’m now looking for an opportunity where I can apply this experience to **[target business problem]** and take on broader responsibilities.
>
> Thank you. I’d be happy to explain any of these projects in more detail.

## Template C — Data Analyst

> Hello, I’m **[Name]**. I’m a **[fresher / experienced] Data Analyst** with experience in **SQL, Python, Excel and Power BI**. My work focuses on turning raw data into clear business insights.
>
> One relevant project was **[project]**, where I worked with **[data source/domain]**. I used **[tools]** for data preparation and analysis and created **[dashboard/report/output]**. The main insight was **[verified insight]**, which helped **[business outcome if applicable]**.
>
> I enjoy working at the intersection of data, problem solving and business communication, and I’m looking forward to applying that approach in this role.

## Template D — Data Scientist / AI

> Hello, I’m **[Name]**, a **[fresher / experienced] Data Scientist** focused on **machine learning and applied analytics**. My practical experience includes **[skills]**.
>
> In **[project]**, the business problem was **[problem]**. I handled **[your contribution]**, including **[data preparation / feature engineering / modelling / evaluation / deployment]**. I compared **[approaches]** and selected **[approach]** based on **[reason]**. The verified evaluation result was **[metric]**.
>
> What I particularly enjoy is understanding the business problem first and then selecting an appropriate analytical solution rather than focusing only on model accuracy.

## Speaking Rules

- Aim for a natural delivery rather than memorization.
- Keep the flow structured and role-relevant.
- Use real metrics only.
- Be prepared to explain every technology you mention.
- If you do not know an answer, acknowledge it professionally and explain how you would approach learning it.
