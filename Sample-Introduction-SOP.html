FRESHER · SAMPLE

Professional Fresher Introduction

Good morning. Thank you for having me.

My name is Aryan Mehta. I'm a fresher Data Scientist, and I'm excited to walk you through my background.

Education: I did my B.E. in Computer Science from PICT Pune and graduated in 2025. Before that, I completed my 12th in the Science stream from the Maharashtra State Board. During college, I developed a strong interest in data and started building practical skills around analytics and machine learning.

Experience: I completed a six-month internship at Hisan Labs Private Limited, where I gained practical exposure to business data, data preparation, modelling and reporting.

What I worked on: I worked on a customer churn prediction use case involving around 2 lakh records. I handled data cleaning, feature engineering, model evaluation and compared Random Forest with XGBoost. I also worked on ETL pipelines using MySQL and Pandas and created Power BI dashboards for weekly sales KPIs.

For my final-year project, I worked on sentiment analysis using BERT on product reviews. I also practised end-to-end workflows through Kaggle projects.

Key skills: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, TensorFlow, PyTorch, HuggingFace, MySQL, Power BI, Excel, Git and Jupyter.

I am now looking for an opportunity where I can apply these practical skills, continue learning and contribute to real business problems. Thank you. I would be happy to explain any project in detail.

EXPERIENCED · 3+ YEARS

Professional Experienced Introduction

Good morning. Thank you for this opportunity. I'll keep my introduction clear and to the point.

My name is Aryan Mehta. I have around three years of experience in the data field. I started as a Data Analyst and moved into a Data Scientist role, working on problems connected to measurable business outcomes.

Current role: I currently work as a Data Scientist in an analytics team. My work involves data extraction, feature engineering, modelling, experiment tracking and communicating insights to business stakeholders.

Current responsibilities: One major use case involved predicting customers likely to default within the next 90 days. I handled the workflow end to end, including SQL extraction, transaction-based feature engineering, model comparison and deployment support. I worked with Logistic Regression, XGBoost and LightGBM and tracked model performance using AUC and other evaluation metrics.

I also worked on customer segmentation using K-Means and RFM-based analysis to support targeted campaigns.

Previous role: Earlier, I worked as a Data Analyst where I built seller performance scorecards, analysed cart abandonment funnels and automated recurring MIS reporting using SQL, Python, Power BI and Tableau.

Key skills: Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, LightGBM, Optuna, MLflow, Power BI, Tableau, Streamlit, MySQL, PySpark and Git.

My approach is to understand the business problem first, identify the right data, build a reliable solution and communicate the outcome clearly. I am looking forward to bringing that approach to this opportunity. Thank you.
