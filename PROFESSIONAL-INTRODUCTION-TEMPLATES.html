Four flexible frameworks designed to help candidates speak clearly without sounding memorised.

01 · FRESHER

Fresher Framework

Good morning. Thank you for the opportunity. My name is [Name]. I recently completed [Degree] from [Institute].

During my academic journey, I developed practical skills in [Python / SQL / ML / BI] and worked on [project/internship area].

In my internship/project, I worked on [problem], handled [data/work], used [tools] and achieved [truthful outcome].

My key skills include [skills]. I am looking for an opportunity where I can apply these skills and contribute to real business problems.

02 · EXPERIENCED

Experienced Professional Framework

Good morning. Thank you for the opportunity. My name is [Name] and I have [X] years of experience in [domain].

Currently, I work as [Role], where I handle [responsibilities] and collaborate with [teams].

My major work includes [problem]. I handled [data/process], used [tools] and delivered [impact].

Previously, I worked on [area], which strengthened my skills in [skills].

I am now looking for an opportunity where I can apply this experience to larger and more complex business problems.

03 · DATA ANALYST

Data Analyst Framework

My name is [Name]. I work in data analytics with a focus on converting business questions into measurable insights.

My regular work includes extracting and transforming data using [SQL/Python], analysing trends, building dashboards using [Power BI/Tableau] and presenting insights to stakeholders.

One example is [problem], where I analysed [dataset], identified [insight] and supported [business outcome].

My key strengths are structured analysis, data visualisation and clear communication of findings.

04 · DATA SCIENCE / AI

Data Science & AI Framework

My name is [Name]. I work in Data Science / AI with practical experience across data preparation, exploratory analysis, machine learning and model evaluation.

I have worked on [use case], where I handled data extraction, preprocessing, feature engineering and model development using [tools].

I compared [models] using [metrics] and focused on both technical performance and business relevance.

I also have exposure to [MLOps/cloud/deployment] where applicable. I am interested in applying this end-to-end approach to real-world problems.
