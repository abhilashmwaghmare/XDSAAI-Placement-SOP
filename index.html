FINAL QUALITY CHECK

Professional standards to keep.

Use these as a practical review before a profile is shared or an interview begins.

01

Accuracy first

Only include experience, tools, certifications, results and metrics you can substantiate.

02

Make it relevant

Prioritize the skills and project work that genuinely match the role being targeted.

03

Stay consistent

Keep role titles, dates, skills and project descriptions aligned across resume, Naukri and LinkedIn.

04

Protect information

Do not share confidential employer, client or personal information in public profiles.
