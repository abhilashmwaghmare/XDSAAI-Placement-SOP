03 · SAMPLE INTRODUCTION

A professional opening that sounds prepared — not memorized.

“

Good morning. Thank you for the opportunity. My name is Aryan Mehta. I’m a fresher Data Scientist with a strong interest in data-driven problem solving. I completed my B.E. in Computer Science and gained practical exposure through a six-month internship, where I worked on data preparation, machine learning and reporting projects.

One of my key projects involved customer churn prediction. I worked with a large dataset, handled preprocessing and feature engineering, evaluated multiple models and documented the final results. I also built ETL workflows and Power BI dashboards to communicate insights clearly.

I’m now looking for an opportunity where I can apply my practical skills, continue learning and contribute to real business problems. Thank you — I’d be happy to explain any project in detail.

04 · PROFESSIONAL STANDARDS

Small details make a profile credible.

The new Do's & Don'ts SOP turns common placement expectations into a quick reference.

✓DO
  • Use real, explainable skills and metrics.
  • Keep resume, LinkedIn and Naukri consistent.
  • Explain your individual project contribution.
  • Listen fully and answer with structure.
  • Tailor your resume to the target role.
×DON'T
  • Do not fabricate experience or certifications.
  • Do not copy another candidate's content.
  • Do not claim tools you cannot explain.
  • Do not publish confidential project information.
  • Do not memorize answers without understanding them.
