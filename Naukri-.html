Headline, Skills & Summary

Resume Headline

Example: Data Science Engineer | Python | Machine Learning | SQL | NLP | Tableau | Power BI

Use the most relevant role and skills rather than a generic statement.

Key Skills

Python, Machine Learning, SQL, Tableau, Power BI, NLP, Deep Learning, Pandas, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, MLOps.

Only list skills you can genuinely discuss.

Profile Summary: use the available space to explain your technical focus, tools, internship/work exposure, project outcomes and target roles. Keep the language specific and evidence-based.

Employment, Education & Preferences

Employment

  • Add relevant internship / experience.
  • Keep duration consistent with your resume.
  • Use responsibilities and DS/ML keywords naturally.

Education

  • Use reverse chronological order.
  • Highlight relevant Computer Science / Statistics / Mathematics background when truthful.

Preferences

  • Select a truthful shift preference.
  • Add locations where you are genuinely willing to work.
  • Do not add unsupported salary expectations.

IT Skills

  • Keep skills aligned with the resume.
  • Keep experience/duration information consistent.
  • Avoid padding the profile with skills you cannot explain.

Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do

  • Complete the profile.
  • Add relevant DS/ML keywords.
  • Keep summary, skills and resume aligned.
  • Add internship accurately.
  • Complete relevant fields.

✕ Don't

  • Avoid “I can” / “I will” capability statements.
  • Do not explain technologies as textbook definitions.
  • Do not mention client names or confidential information.
  • Do not duplicate content unnecessarily.
  • Do not add fake skills, experience or credentials.
