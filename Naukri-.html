Profile Summary Formula

1 · Identity

State degree/experience level and target domain.

2 · Expertise

Highlight relevant technical skills and practical exposure.

3 · Evidence

Mention projects, internship/work and measurable contributions where genuine.

4 · Direction

State the role family you are seeking and the value you can contribute.

Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do

  • Complete all relevant sections.
  • Verify contact information.
  • Upload the latest resume.
  • Use relevant role and skill keywords.
  • Keep Naukri, LinkedIn and resume consistent.
  • Update the profile whenever important details change.

✕ Don't

  • Do not add fake skills or experience.
  • Do not use another person's resume or profile details.
  • Do not add confidential client information.
  • Do not exaggerate project outcomes.
  • Do not leave major sections incomplete.
  • Do not use irrelevant keywords just to improve search visibility.
