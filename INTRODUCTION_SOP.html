01 · CORE INTERVIEW SOP

Introduction SOP

A structured framework for delivering a confident, relevant and professional Data Science / AI interview introduction.

5+ minute flowRole-focusedProfessional EnglishNo client/project names

Introduction Structure

Follow this order so the interviewer can understand your profile quickly and naturally.

#ComponentWhat to cover
01NameState your full name clearly. Skip it if the interviewer has already introduced you.
02EducationPresent qualifications in reverse chronological order, most recent first.
03ExperienceExplain work history from the most recent role backwards.
04Roles & ResponsibilitiesCore section. Explain what you actually did: data analysed, models built, pipelines designed, tools used and insights generated.
05Key SkillsMention relevant technical skills and tools concisely without teaching the technology.

What to Exclude

REMOVE

Hobbies

Keep the opening role-focused. Personal interests can be discussed when specifically asked.

REMOVE

Strengths & Weaknesses

Reserve this for a dedicated HR question instead of volunteering it in the introduction.

REMOVE

Family Details

Family background is not part of a professional Data Science introduction.

REMOVE

Goals

Long-term and short-term goals should be discussed when specifically asked.

Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do

  • Use past tense or present continuous tense.
  • Keep the introduction to a minimum of 5 minutes.
  • Focus primarily on roles and responsibilities.
  • Speak with clarity, confidence and a controlled pace.

✕ Don't

  • Do not use “I can…”, “I will…” or “I want to…” as capability statements.
  • Do not explain what a technology is; explain what you did with it.
  • Do not use client names or project names.
  • Do not divide the story into tool-by-tool explanations.
Key Reminder
Minimum 5 minutes · Focus on WHAT YOU DID · Use past tense or present continuous · No client/project names.
