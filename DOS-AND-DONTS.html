Do's & Don'ts

A single visual quality gate for professional introductions, resumes, profiles, projects and interview communication.

ProfessionalEvidence-basedConsistentInterview-ready

Professional Communication

✓ DO

  • Speak clearly and at a controlled pace.
  • Use past tense or present continuous for actual work.
  • Explain what you did, not what a technology means.
  • Use measurable outcomes only when genuine.
  • Keep the conversation structured and role-focused.

✕ DON'T

  • Do not use “I can”, “I will” or “I want to” as substitutes for evidence.
  • Do not over-explain basic technologies.
  • Do not disclose confidential information.
  • Do not invent metrics, projects or experience.
  • Do not memorise a script so rigidly that it sounds unnatural.

Resume Standards

✓ DO

  • Keep roles and responsibilities central.
  • Use reverse chronological order.
  • Include relevant technical skills.
  • Keep claims specific and verifiable.
  • Maintain consistency across resume, LinkedIn and Naukri.

✕ DON'T

  • Do not use client/project names when the SOP requires them to be excluded.
  • Do not write generic “hardworking” statements without evidence.
  • Do not add skills you cannot discuss.
  • Do not use duplicated content.
  • Do not let a template dictate facts.

Final Professional Gate

Before placement submission: Resume reviewed · LinkedIn reviewed · Naukri reviewed · Introduction practised · Documents consistent · Claims verified · Confidential information removed.
Red flags: fake experience, unsupported metrics, copied personal content, inconsistent dates, unexplained skills, confidential project/client details or generic AI-generated statements presented as personal experience.
