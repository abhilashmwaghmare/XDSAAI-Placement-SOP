Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do

  • Complete every important section.
  • Use a keyword-rich but truthful headline.
  • Keep resume, GitHub and LinkedIn consistent.
  • Use measurable achievements where genuine.
  • Show projects and proof of work.
  • Review public visibility before sharing the profile.

✕ Don't

  • Do not invent experience, projects or certifications.
  • Do not expose confidential client information.
  • Do not keyword-stuff irrelevant technologies.
  • Do not use a casual or poor-quality profile photo.
  • Do not publish phone/email unnecessarily in public sections.
  • Do not copy another candidate's profile.
