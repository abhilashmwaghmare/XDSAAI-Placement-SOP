## Template 04 — Project-Focused Resume

Use this when practical projects are stronger than formal experience.

1. Header + role title
2. Professional summary
3. Technical skills
4. 3–4 strongest projects
5. Internship / training
6. Education
7. Certifications

For every project use: **Problem → Contribution → Technology → Approach → Result → Learning**.

## Resume Review Checklist

- One clear target role
- Strong summary
- Relevant skills only
- Projects explain contribution, not just features
- Metrics are genuine
- Dates are consistent
- No grammar or formatting errors
- Links work
- Resume matches LinkedIn and Naukri
