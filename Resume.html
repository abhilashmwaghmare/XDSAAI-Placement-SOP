Resume Preparation SOP

Build a clean, role-focused resume where roles and responsibilities are the core evidence of your technical profile.

ATS-friendly structureReverse chronologicalImpact-focusedTruthful content

Mandatory Resume Structure

#SectionDetails
01Title / HeaderFull name, email, mobile number and target designation such as Data Science Engineer, Data Analyst or ML Engineer.
02Objective / GoalOptional. Keep it concise at 1–2 lines and align it with the target DS/ML role.
03ExperienceReverse chronological order: company, designation, duration and the actual responsibilities handled.
04EducationCourse, stream, completion year and relevant academic project.
05CertificationsCertificate name, ID where available and year/validity.
06Key SkillsKnown technical skills across Python, SQL, ML/DL, BI, cloud and frameworks.
07Personal DetailsOptional. Keep concise and include only information appropriate for the application.

How to Write Responsibilities

ROLE EVIDENCE

Problem handledData usedWork performedTools appliedOutcome / impact

Data Science Engineer

Roles & Responsibilities

Built and evaluated classification models using structured customer data, performed preprocessing and feature engineering, and documented model performance using appropriate evaluation metrics.

Automated recurring data preparation workflows and developed dashboards to communicate business insights to stakeholders.

Collaborated with cross-functional teams to translate business requirements into measurable analytical outputs.

Core principle: describe what you did with the technology. Do not turn the resume into a textbook explaining what Python, SQL or machine learning means.

Resume Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do

  • Use past tense or present continuous tense.
  • Add all relevant technical skills.
  • Keep roles and responsibilities as the core focus.
  • Mention certifications with ID and validity when available.
  • Keep personal details concise and optional.

✕ Don't

  • Do not use “I can”, “I will” or “I want to”.
  • Do not explain technologies.
  • Do not use client or project names.
  • Do not write “know”, “learnt” or “familiar with” as responsibility evidence.
  • Do not use generic AI-generated content as a substitute for real work.
Key Reminder: Roles & Responsibilities = core of the resume · Past/present-continuous tense · Relevant technical skills · No client/project names · No capability-only statements.
